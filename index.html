Add to your home screen
Follow these steps to install this as an app on your phone. It will work offline once installed.
iPhone (Safari)
1
Open this file in Safari — it must be Safari, not Chrome.
2
Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3
Scroll down in the share sheet and tap "Add to Home Screen".
4
Tap "Add" in the top right. Done — the app icon appears on your home screen.
Android (Chrome)
1
Open this file in Chrome.
2
Tap the three-dot menu in the top right corner.
3
Tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
4
Tap "Add" to confirm. The app icon appears on your home screen.
Once installed, the app opens full screen with no browser bar and works completely offline. Your progress is saved automatically on your device.
